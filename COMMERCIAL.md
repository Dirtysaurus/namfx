Third-party components keep their own licenses (see [THIRD_PARTY.md](THIRD_PARTY.md)).

Content loaded at runtime is not part of namfx and is not distributed with it. This covers NAM
models (`.nam`), impulse responses, LV2 plugins and effect-type packs made by other people. Their
own licenses apply.
